  Universities manage rooms, labs and equipment through disconnected processes: bookings

  reported problem to who fixed it. Nothing is auditable and nothing surfaces overdue
  maintenance until someone complains.

  I contributed to a single web platform covering both halves of the problem — facility
  and asset booking, and maintenance incidents from fault report through technician
  update to resolution — with role-based access control so each user sees and does only
  what their role permits, and an audit trail over every state change.

## Why this project is on the portfolio

Two reasons beyond the technical work. First, it is the one project that demonstrates
working inside a team rather than alone, with shared conventions and a codebase I did
not originate. Second, it is the only repository here that outside developers have
starred and forked, which is the closest thing to independent validation this profile
has.

It is also the widest technology spread on the profile — a typed backend with an ORM and
relational database, rather than a notebook — which rounds out work that is otherwise
predominantly Python and data tooling.

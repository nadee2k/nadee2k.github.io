## Description is not explanation

Most retail reporting stops at describing what happened. The step that adds value is
asking whether a difference is real — a revenue dip in one region might be noise, or it
might be a genuine signal, and the two demand completely different responses. Hypothesis
testing is what separates them before a regression model is fitted to explain the effect.
